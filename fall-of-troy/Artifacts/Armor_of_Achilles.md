# Armor of Achilles

## Basic Information
- **Type:** Armor
- **Current Owner:** [[Humans/Achilles]]
- **Forged By:** Hephaestus, god of the forge, at Thetis's request
- **Abilities:** Divine protection, Recognition on the battlefield, Representation of the world's totality

## Description & History
The original armour of Achilles was taken from the body of Patroclus by Hector after he killed him — armour that Achilles had lent to his companion so he might be mistaken for the great warrior and drive the Trojans from the ships. Its loss was both a practical problem and a devastating insult.

Thetis went to Hephaestus, the divine smith, and asked him to forge a replacement. He worked through the night. The result was a shield of extraordinary workmanship — Homer devotes nearly a hundred lines of the Iliad to describing it — on which Hephaestus depicted the entire world: two cities, one at peace and one at war; the heavens and the earth; harvest scenes and festivals and the ocean running around the rim. The shield was not merely armour; it was a cosmological statement, a reminder that the small brutal war on the plain of Troy existed inside a larger world that went on regardless.

The new armour allowed Achilles to return to battle and gave him the divine protection that the original set had always carried. It did not protect his heel.
