# Trojans

## Basic Information
- **Type:** Military, Political, Royal
- **Outcome:** Defeated
- **Leader:** [[Humans/Hector]]
- **Headquarters:** [[Locations/Troy]]

## Description & History
The Trojans were the defenders of Ilium and its allied cities, led in the field by Hector and governed by King Priam. Unlike the Greeks, who were a coalition of distant kingdoms united by oath, the Trojans were fighting for their home — their walls, their families, the city they could see from the battlefield. This gave them cohesion the Greeks sometimes lacked, and a ferocity born of necessity.

They held out for ten years. Against a coalition that outnumbered them, that had stripped their allied cities one by one, that contained the greatest warrior alive — they held their walls for a decade. They came within reach of burning the Greek ships in Year 9. They could have won.

Their defeat came not from failure of courage but from a failure of judgment: they brought the horse inside. The priests and Cassandra warned against it. Cassandra had always warned against everything, and had never been believed — that was her curse, given by Apollo when she refused him. The Trojans opened their gates.
