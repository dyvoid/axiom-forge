# Ajax

## Basic Information
- **Sex:** Male
- **Born:** c. 1245 BCE
- **Died:** Year 10 of the Siege of Troy
- **Allegiance:** [[Factions/Greeks]]

## Description & History
The largest man in the Greek camp and the only one who never once received divine assistance, Ajax fought the entire war on his own strength. He carried a tower shield of seven ox-hides and a layer of bronze, and behind it he was effectively immovable — the Greeks used him the way a wall is used. When the Trojans reached the ships and set the first hull alight, it was Ajax who held the deck alone, retreating one plank at a time and giving ground more slowly than any man there could have.

He was not clever and never pretended to be. His duel with Hector lasted a full day and ended in a draw and an exchange of gifts, which is as close as the Iliad comes to admitting that two men can be evenly matched. He recovered Achilles' body from the field under fire, which no one else attempted.

Then the armour of Achilles was awarded — not to the man who had carried the body out, but to Odysseus, who had argued for it better. Ajax, who had never lost a fight and had just lost a debate, went out that night intending to kill the men who had judged against him, was driven mad by Athena before he could, and slaughtered a herd of cattle instead. He came back to himself at dawn, surrounded by what he had done, and fell on his own sword. He is the only major Greek to be destroyed by something other than a Trojan.
