# Death of Hector

## Basic Information
- **Type:** Battle, Personal
- **Date:** Year 10 of the Siege
- **Location:** [[Locations/Troy]]

## Description & History
[[Humans/Achilles|Achilles]] entered battle in new armour forged by Hephaestus — divine work commissioned by [[Gods/Thetis|Thetis]], its surface worked with images of the world, the sea, the stars, the harvests of men. The Trojans broke before him. He killed without stopping. The river Scamander swelled with bodies and complained.

[[Humans/Hector|Hector]] waited outside the Scaean Gate. [[Humans/Priam|Priam]] and Hecuba begged him from the walls to come inside. He stood and thought it through — everything he had done, every choice, every day of the war — and when Achilles appeared, he ran. He circled the city three times. Then he stopped and turned.

He asked Achilles for a pledge: that the loser's body would be returned to his family. Achilles refused. They fought. [[Gods/Athena|Athena]], having disguised herself as Hector's brother to lure him into the fight, vanished at the moment of engagement, leaving Hector alone. Achilles found the gap in the armour at the throat and drove his spear through it.

He dragged Hector's body behind his chariot around the walls of Troy while Priam and Hecuba and Andromache watched from above. He did it every day for twelve days. The gods eventually intervened — even Achilles had to stop somewhere — and Zeus sent Priam to the Greek camp to beg. The body was returned. Troy held a funeral for eleven days.
