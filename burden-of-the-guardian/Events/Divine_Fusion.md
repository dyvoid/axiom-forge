## Basic Information
- **Type:** Supernatural, Cosmic
- **Start Date:** 1453 BCE
- **Locations:** [[Locations/Crete]]

## Description & History
Ylverian merged with Thalirin to lead a counterattack against the Mycenaeans. The fusion
unleashed devastating power but left both beings altered. Ylverian severed the connection
in the aftermath of atrocities Thalirin could not undo.
