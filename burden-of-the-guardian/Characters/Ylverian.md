## Description & History
An ancient cosmic being from the Unbound Realm. In 1453 BCE, Ylverian merged with
Thalirin to lead a counterattack against the Mycenaeans. The fusion was severed after
atrocities followed; the trauma of that severance shaped both beings for what remained
of Thalirin's life.

## Personality
Patient on a cosmic timescale; reckless on a human one. Believes love is a form of
guardianship, and guardianship a form of violence.
